What is LinkedIn Optimization?

Before they meet you, they search for you. Recruiters, lecturers and collaborators look you up online first, so your profile often makes the first impression. Optimizing LinkedIn means making your profile complete, consistent and searchable, so it works for you even when you are not in the room.

1. Build profiles that work for you

Make LinkedIn, and the sites your field checks, complete, consistent and searchable. Use a clear headline, a short About section and results-focused experience.

2. Craft a coherent identity

Tell one clear, honest story (strengths, audience, proof, direction) the same way everywhere you appear online.

3. Manage your digital footprint

Google yourself in a private window, then sort what you find into what to keep, remove or lock down.

Being invisible costs you almost as much as being careless.

Cleaning Up Your Digital Footprint

The Three Reader Test: would I be comfortable if a recruiter, my lecturer and my grandmother all read this?

Keep & Amplify

Project write-ups, awards, talks, research, volunteering, articles you wrote.

Remove

Offensive jokes, rants about employers or lecturers, confidential information.

Lock

Old public photos, inactive accounts, mismatched names or photos, outdated bios.

Your 7-Day Plan

One small action a day. By the end of the week, your brand says what you mean.

Day 1

Write your one-line brand statement

Use the formula: I help [audience] to [outcome] by using [strengths], and I'm growing toward [direction].

Day 2

Ask three people for three words

Ask three people to give you three words that describe you.

Day 3

Rewrite your headline and About

Use skills that repeat in job posts you would love.

Day 4

Add Featured work

Showcase your proof, then request one recommendation.

Day 5

Google yourself

Search in a private window and delete what needs to be deleted.

Day 6

Post something

Share something you built or learned.

Day 7

Review

Check that your story is consistent everywhere and note what to improve next.

Your brand is already out there.
Make it say what you mean.
